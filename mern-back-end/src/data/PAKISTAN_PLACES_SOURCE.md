# Pakistan populated places dataset

`pakistan-populated-places.json` is a snapshot of the GeoNames Pakistan country dump, filtered to populated-place feature codes and reduced to unique primary and ASCII names. Downloaded 2026-11-03.

Source: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/)

GeoNames data is provided under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. The source describes its data as provided as-is and may contain incomplete or outdated place names. Customers can enter a city or town manually when it is not listed.
